# Privacy Policy

Zenlessentials is a free, unofficial Zenless Zone Zero story tracker, game database, and squad builder. You can use it as a guest. Signing in is optional and lets you sync saves across devices.

## Sign-in information

**Google sign-in.** When you choose Continue with Google, Supabase uses Google OAuth to verify your identity. The basic profile and email permissions can provide your Google account identifier, email address and verification status, name, and profile image URL. Supabase stores provider identity information and profile metadata with your authentication record. Zenlessentials uses the account identity to associate your saves with you and displays your email on the Account page. It does not use your Google name or profile image in the interface.

**Email sign-in.** The email address you enter is sent to Supabase to create or find an account and deliver a sign-in link or, where available, a verification code. The app uses passwordless sign-in. It does not ask for your Google password.

**Google user data.** The app uses Google user data for sign-in and to give you access to your synced saves. The browser can retain the Supabase session, including associated profile metadata, to keep you signed in. Supabase processes the Google identity as our authentication and storage provider. We do not send your saves to Google or use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or sale. The app does not access Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendars, or other Google services through your account. Zenlessentials’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

## Saves, browser storage, and offline use

- **Guest saves** stay in the local storage of the browser or installed web app you use. They include story progress, your collection and personal tiers, squad names and lineups you enter, and preferences such as theme and spoiler settings. Guest saves are uploaded only if you choose **Import guest saves** while signed in.
- **Account saves** are stored in Supabase under your account ID, with a revision number to coordinate edits. The synced snapshot includes your story completions and their timestamps, collection, favorites, wishlist, personal tiers, squads, and preferences. The database’s access rules restrict normal signed-in users to their own saves. The app keeps a local copy and syncs changes when you reconnect.
- **Local storage and offline cache** are scoped to the site address and browser context. The app stores session information locally for sign-in and uses a service worker and browser caches to keep app files, visited game data, and artwork available offline. Clearing site data can remove local saves, cached account data, the sign-in session, and offline files. Guest saves can be transferred with the separate story and collection JSON backups.

## Hosting, service providers, and tracking

Zenlessentials does not install advertising or analytics scripts or set its own tracking cookies. Hosting and sign-in providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request or security logs as part of serving and protecting their services. Provider cookies may apply when you use their services, such as on Google’s sign-in page.

- **Cloudflare** serves the official site at zenlessentials.org and the legacy Cloudflare Pages address. [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)
- **Netlify** hosts the legacy Netlify address. [Netlify Privacy Statement](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)
- **Supabase** handles sign-in, email delivery through its configured mail service, and storage of authentication records and synchronized saves. [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Google** handles Google sign-in if you choose it. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **GitHub** hosts the public repository and issue tracker. If you post an issue, GitHub handles that information and the post is public. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Retention, deletion, and your choices

Guest saves and local copies can remain on your device until you remove them or the browser clears site data. Signing out ends the local sign-in session and can leave a local save copy. Changes you make with the tracker and collection controls while signed in can sync to your account. Clearing site data for an address or installation removes its local copies, while your Supabase account and cloud saves remain. You can export story progress and collection data before clearing it.

The app has no automatic expiration for account records or cloud saves and no in-app account deletion control. You can request deletion of your account. Removing the account from Supabase also deletes its active cloud save row. Backup and operational log retention depends on the provider and service settings. You can also revoke Zenlessentials’ access under [connections in your Google Account](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revoking Google access does not itself delete the Zenlessentials account or its saves.

## Privacy requests

To request access, correction, or deletion, or to ask about other privacy rights that apply to you, email [support@zenlessentials.org](mailto:support@zenlessentials.org). We use your email address and the details you share to respond to your request. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting on it. Keep sign-in links, verification codes, and save backups out of your message.
