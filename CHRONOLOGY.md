## Placement conventions

The game’s prerequisite is recorded separately from the tracker’s suggested sequence. Where early access was offered, the standard narrative route remains the default. Some launch conditions changed during later updates, so historical requirements are labeled when known. Empty unlock fields are displayed as unverified.

| Confidence | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Official recommendation | A publisher explicitly states a best-experience chapter or a required story link that determines this placement. |
| Continuity-based | Archive order, relationships, locations, introductions, or dialogue establish the suggested position. |
| Flexible | The source confirms the story, while its exact timing between major chapters is open. |

The tracker treats the first incomplete core story as the primary recommendation. Optional content appears between chapters and can accompany the next core story. Completion of an optional story does not gate the core route.

## Enigma of the Labyrinth

**Joint Investigation: The Divine Maze** introduces the investigation in Papago Hollow and precedes **Enigma of the Labyrinth: Operation Bagel**. The Operation Bagel playthrough returns to Nanson and Penny and supplies continuity evidence. Its slot after The Long Goodbye is a playable Season 3 default. Neither the mode unlock nor its chronology requires a player to mark the expired event complete.

The eight independently checkable original commissions are **Labyrinth Commission**, **Bangboo Lost**, **Disappearing Eous**, **Exploring the Lower-Level**, **The Great Aquarium Rescue**, **The Hematite Mystery**, **Final Rescue**, and **Behind the Plan**. The public game-data changelog has variant text for the hematite objective. The tracker uses the short, first-season commission label. Investigation stages, infrastructure upgrades, and side requests can occur inside the chain. Later **Operation Bagel II** and **Spooky Trial** updates changed the mode’s gameplay and seasonal commissions. Their status as direct story sequels is unverified.

## Availability and maintenance

- **Permanent** means the story or commission remains accessible. **Archived / replayable** means an expired event can still be played in-game. The old Ridu Chronicles list moved under **Events → Polychrome Progress → Polychrome Guide**. Arpeggio Fault’s separate move to a Side Mission is recorded on its card. [2.5 update](https://zenless.hoyoverse.com/en-us/news/161728) · [2.8 update](https://zenless.hoyoverse.com/en-us/news/163836)
- **Limited time** uses the latest published server cutoff across regions for display. Consult the in-game timer for that server’s exact cutoff. **Expired** means the original event window has closed and the sources do not list a replay path. **Availability unknown** means sources were insufficient to establish current access. Regional availability, individual archive transitions, patch-adjusted unlocks, and late-added event branches need periodic rechecking.
- **Data validation** rejects missing required content and source fields, duplicate IDs and orders, missing references, and invalid child relationships. Strictly ordered chronological edges also reject circular dependencies. It validates structure, while the sources and these notes document the research judgment.
